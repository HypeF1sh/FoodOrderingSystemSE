# Landing page photos

Up to 5 photos named `hero-1.jpg` ... `hero-5.jpg` (or `.webp`). The home page picks them up automatically and
crossfades through them in that order. With no photos, the hero shows soft coloured slides instead.
`credits.json` holds the credit line shown on each photo; keep it in step when a photo changes.

- Landscape, 1920 × 1080, about 300 KB each.
- Free licence only, and credit each photo in the report's references.

All five are from Unsplash under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) (free to use, no permission
needed). Downloaded 5 Nov 2026 at 1920 × 1080.
